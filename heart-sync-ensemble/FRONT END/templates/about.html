Traditional machine learning models often struggle with imbalanced datasets and lack interpretability, limiting their clinical applicability. To address these challenges, this research introduces HeartSync Ensemble, a comprehensive framework that combines advanced ensemble learning techniques, SMOTE for data balancing, XAI for interpretability, and K-best feature selection for dimensionality reduction. The framework incorporates stacking and voting classifiers, alongside a novel CNN+RF hybrid model, evaluated using K-fold cross-validation. By comparing model performance before and after K-best feature selection, this study aims to develop a highly accurate and interpretable system for heart disease prediction suitable for real-world medical applications.
